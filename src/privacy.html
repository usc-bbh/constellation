Constellation Your STARS report, mapped

Privacy policy

Constellation reads your STARS report on your own device. Your report is never sent to us or anyone else.

Effective 4 November 2026. Covers the Constellation website (usc-bbh.github.io/constellation) and the Constellation Chrome extension.

The Chrome extension

  • It reads a tab only when you click its toolbar button, and only the tab you clicked it on. It has no access to any other tab or site, and reads nothing in the background.
  • When you click the button, it looks for STARS report text on that page and opens a summary in a new tab. The text is passed to that tab once, held in memory, and dropped. Nothing is saved.
  • It makes no network requests. It has no analytics, no tracking and no ads.
  • It asks for two permissions: activeTab, to read the tab you clicked the button on, and scripting, to run the code that finds the report text in that tab.

The website

  • When you upload a STARS report PDF, your browser reads it. The file is not uploaded anywhere. Your name, student ID and mailing address are stripped before the report is read.
  • The website may count visits, summaries made and star ratings with GoatCounter, a simple visit counter. It sends only those fixed labels, never anything from your report, and uses no cookies. Like any website request, GoatCounter receives your IP address and browser type, which it uses only to count unique visits.

Feedback

The feedback page shows a Google Form run by Google. Google sees what you type there and may set cookies. Your answers go to the BUAI Builder Hub team. Please don't include your student ID or your report.

What we never do

We don't collect, store, sell or share your STARS report or anything in it. We don't use it for anything other than showing you your summary, and we don't build profiles or show ads.

Contact

Questions: bbh@usc.edu. Constellation is built by BUAI Builder Hub, a student group. It is not an official USC system.
